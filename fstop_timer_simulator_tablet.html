Proposed dial behaviour

  • Turn: change the selected value by one step size per click.
  • Press: choose what the dial changes: stops, base time, then step size.
  • Touch: the Start / Pause / Resume button at the bottom.
  • Drag the ring, use the mouse wheel, or the left and right arrow keys to turn it.
  • While an exposure runs, the display shows the countdown and a progress ring, and turning is ignored.

Tablet setup (Galaxy Tab S9 FE)

Tick items as you set up the real tablet. The first three also change the simulated screen above, so you can see what goes wrong without them.

    Messages between the units

    Status updates arrive about ten times a second and are not listed.

      Things to try
      1. On the Time tab, press a start-time preset, change the step size, then press Start. Set the speed to 5× to watch a long exposure quickly.
      2. On Strip test, run the test and watch the enlarger switch off between steps while the next strip to cover is shown. Tap a strip afterwards and choose Use selected strip.
      3. On Recipe, add a burn and a dodge and run it. Dodge hold times count down during the main exposure.
      4. Start an exposure and switch the Bluetooth link off. The relay finishes the exposure and the screen catches up when the link returns.
      5. Switch focus light on, then cut the link. The relay switches the enlarger off after the heartbeat is lost.
      6. Use the foot pedal with the screen on a different tab. It still starts the last armed exposure.
      7. Open the Dial UI view and turn the dial. Press it to change what it adjusts.
      8. In Tablet setup, switch off the red film, fullscreen or Do Not Disturb and see what the screen would look like. Send a test notification with Do Not Disturb off.
      9. Cut the Bluetooth link and restore it. With Web app you must tap Reconnect and use the bright pairing dialog; with Wrapped app it reconnects by itself.
      10. Meter tab: press Start metering. The enlarger comes on and the safelight goes off. Watch the status say Settling... then Steady. In the bench panel, move the probe between the highlight and shadow areas and store each reading. Change the lamp warm-up, probe noise or negative range and see how the status and the readings react.
      11. Press Zero probe with the probe dark level set to something other than 0.03 lx. The enlarger stays off while the dark offset is read.
      12. Calibrate tab: for grade 2, store a light level (set the bench to a blank baseboard first: untick Negative in the carrier). Set a base time on the Time tab, then Set up strip test, and run it at 10×. Judge the developed strips in the bench panel by eye and pick one. Do both the highlight and shadow strips, then do a second grade such as 4. If every strip is too light or too dark, press Redo and change the base time.
      13. Open Check your calibration at the bottom of the bench panel to see how close your picks were to the simulated paper's hidden response. Smaller strip steps give a closer match.
      14. With the negative back in, store a highlight and shadow reading at a grade between the two you calibrated (for example 3), then open the Print tab. Change the highlight placement and the time and watch the H and S markers move. Press Use as base time, expose, and compare the developed print in the bench panel with the prediction.
      15. Develop tab: with the temperature probe plugged in on the bench, move the developer temperature slider and watch the reading and the notes change. Shorten the baths with the minus buttons, press Start, and follow the countdown, the cues and the Pause and Next bath buttons. Turn on a time change per degree to see the develop time adjust.
      16. Compare the tone scale with the red film on and off (Tablet setup), and during an exposure, when it turns red.
      17. Lower the brightness in focus mode with the red film off. With Wrapped app the glow dims; with Web app it does not.
